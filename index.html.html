Reflective first, instructive second

Seven values. Six areas of school life. Forty-two considerations of how AI interacts with schools in Tower Hamlets.

AI technology is increasingly interacting with all domains of school life. As a borough we believe that working with this technology should be values-driven. To understand the opportunities and challenges AI presents, this framework makes those interactions visible and offers the questions a leadership team should sit with — rather than a mandate. It is a practical starting point for considering AI, grounded in the reality of running a school in Tower Hamlets.

How the two halves fit

The matrix is the stable framework — Layers 1 and 2, reviewed every year or two. Its questions are designed to be asked in SLT meetings, governor sessions and staff CPD; the practical points beneath them are where reflection turns into a decision.

The Radar is Layer 3: the volatile material, watched and summarised so the framework never has to be rewritten just because a new tool or update landed. Every Radar item is tagged against the same six domains as the matrix, so a head can move from "what changed this week" straight to "which of our commitments it touches."
