AI Baseline School self-assessment
Tower Hamlets Education Partnership

Where is your school on AI, and what should you do next?

A fifteen-minute self-assessment across the six areas of school life the AI Compass covers. It produces a profile of where you are now, and a short list of what to do about it, ordered by what matters most.

  • 1Safety and compliance first. Seven statutory basics, as a checklist. These are not developmental — they apply now, so they are scored separately from everything else.
  • 2Six areas of school life. Curriculum, teaching, safeguarding, academic outcomes, culture and inclusion, each scored from 1 to 4.
  • 3A check on the picture. Two questions about how confident you are that the answers reflect what actually happens.
  • 4Your profile and next steps. Generated immediately, and printable.
Nothing you enter leaves this page
There is no account, no database and no submission. Everything is worked out in your browser, and closing the tab discards it. Print or save your report before you leave.

It works best as a leadership team conversation rather than a solo task. One person clicks, but the value is in where colleagues disagree. There are no right answers, and an honest "not yet" is more useful than an optimistic "embedded".

About the Compass
Part 1 of 3 — Safety and compliance

The statutory floor

This section covers what schools should already have in place as of September 2026. These are statutory and regulatory requirements rather than developmental steps, and they should be addressed before considering wider implementation.

Tick what is in place today, not what is planned. If you are not certain, leave it unticked: an honest gap is more useful than an assumption.

Part 2 of 3 — The six areas of school life

Where are you now?

The AI Compass maps six areas of school life against seven values THEP schools hold about educating young people. These six questions ask where your school currently sits in each area.

Most schools sit at 1 or 2 across much of this. That is the expected picture, not a failing.

THEP AI Baseline. A self-assessment instrument, not an inspection framework and not a measure of school quality. It is designed to start a leadership conversation and to point towards the next useful action.

Actions are grounded in current statutory guidance and in practitioner research, including Human Intelligence First: the Tool is the Smallest Part (Educate Ventures Research with Cambridge OCR and the Good Future Foundation, October 2026). Sources are named in each section so you can check them.

Nothing entered here is transmitted or stored. Verify every regulatory point at source before acting on it.
